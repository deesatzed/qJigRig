## Inputs and actions still needed

- An independent reviewer and separately held reference material are needed for a real pilot. Use the frozen-set and review commands in [README.md](README.md#frozen-question-sets); do not treat developer smoke judgments as independent labels.
- The practical broad-research route needs an explicitly authorized web diagnostic and spending acknowledgment, plus a configured positive `OPENROUTER_WEB_TOOL_BUDGET_USD`. A previous call exceeded its requested search count, so this setting is not a guaranteed spending cap. No such diagnostic is implied by the existing bounded-source smoke tests.
- The 100-case limit supports the protocol's pilot-size range, but the completed 100-case exercise used simulated responses. A real workload requires its own authorized call and spending budget, independent review and measured operational evidence.
- Confirmatory non-inferiority requires a separately frozen holdout, prespecified margin, paired uncertainty method, coverage guard and adequate sample size. Current scores are exploratory and do not establish that research claim.
